## Visual system

One system, since #234: Instrument's structure carrying Organic's warmth. The design pass (#326 to #355) put every screen on it and wrote its ten principles into DESIGN.md's "What this pass enforces"; `docs/look/audit/` holds that pass's before and after for every screen, both themes. Round 2 (#358 to #375), on Timothy's "even if they were my calls, if its bad change it" (6 Oct), took the moves the pass had kept his calls against; its before and after are in `docs/look/audit/round2/`.

- **The ground is plain since #289**, Timothy's call. Light: ground #ffffff, surface #f4f4f4 (controls, tiles, inputs, the introduction's answer rows), raised #ffffff (cards, told apart by shadow and hairline). Dark: #121212, #1c1c1c, #262626. Shadows are neutral black in both themes; the accents and the ink stay warm.
- **One card, one lifted thing** (#328). `.card` is the only card recipe: raised fill, 1px `card-edge`, radius 16, shadow-1, and in dark a sheen (raised plus 4.6% white over the top 88px) because a black shadow vanishes on #121212; a card that takes another fill adds `bg-none`. Each screen has at most one `elev-2`, the card holding its one tap (Today's floor card, Practice's stage, Lessons' Up next). No card inside a card, no filled tile inside a card. Role materials stay their own: `.card-stage` (the stage, on the surface step in dark), `.card-premium` (plum), `.inset-group`. Today's practice card is its one box (#500): the games' rows, the foot, the tester's tile and the freeze's note stand on the ground.
- **Border jobs.** `card-edge` (.09/.12) is a card's hairline; `edge` (.14/.16) is a rule, a connector, an input's boundary, and the answer rows' 2px edge and 4px lip; `hairline` (.08) separates list rows; `ink-edge` (the other theme's card-edge, .12/.09, and .34/.32 under Increase Contrast, #499) is the hairline on an ink fill: the toast's capsule, AuthNote and Undo.
- **Shapes.** Radius control 12, card 16, sheet 20 (the paywall, Practice's stage and /boss's headliner). Chips are the only pills, Record the only circle. Bars are square (except a trait's level meter, #482: rounded, its track ink at 13%, edged under Increase Contrast), trough (`sand`, #e4e4e4 light, #333333 dark) and fill. Where the browser draws it (Chromium today, never an iPhone), a surface's corner is continuous since #437: `corner-shape: superellipse(1.5)` with each surface radius drawn 1.35 times larger so the roundness holds, in one `@supports` block at the end of `app/globals.css`; pills and Record stay round, and `lib/corners.test.ts` keeps the block in step with the radii above (since #500 it holds `.home-mode` and its `::before`, not `.home-tile` or `.home-seg`). `--color-trough` is read by `.segmented` only.
- **Colour.** **The tap has no colour of its own (#459, Timothy 12 Nov: "8 for home and 10 for feedback").** One filled tap per screen, with no hover step (#327; `.press` veils it on every pointer, and each fill sets its own veil). ## Tokens (`app/globals.css`, `lib/motion.ts`, `lib/ui.ts`)

- **Type** (#331). Outfit 600/700/800 for numbers and UI, Figtree for body and Demos's voice. Text: `title` 26/700 (-0.02em, #332; the recorder's topic at rest is `min(26px, 6.667vw)`, sized to the screen as Today's is, #451), `detail` 17/700, `read` 16 (reading paragraphs only), `body` 15, `row` 14/700, `link` 13/600, `caption` 13, `suffix` 11/700 (the floor, #450: nothing a person reads is under 11px, the paywall stair's superscript cents excepted, `lib/type-floor.test.ts`); large titles 34/800 and `.section-head` 20/800 come from their components. Numbers at 800 tabular: `num-xl` 72, `num-hero` 56, `num-l` 36, `num-m` 24, `num-s` 17. `lib/type-scale.test.ts` counts arbitrary `text-[Npx]` and `text-[Nrem]` sizes and may only go down (1, the splash wordmark). Heavy letters (700 and over) are tracked no tighter than -0.015em at 34px and over and -0.012em under it, the point past which Outfit's pairs touch (#454, `lib/type-tracking.test.ts`, measured by `scripts/look-kerning.mjs`); numbers keep their own, and the roulette's reel is the one exception. A title takes no full stop (`lib/title.ts`, COPY-RULES). Headings balance, paragraphs wrap pretty, body sets `text-rendering: geometricPrecision`.
- **Components layer** (`@layer components`, so a colour utility beats it without `!`): `.eyebrow` (Outfit 13/600 stone-500, sentence case), `.detail-head` (17/700, a head one step in), `.section-head-count` (15/700 stone-400 after a section title), `.text-link` (13/600 stone-500, a 44px hit area from its own `::after`; the door you came for adds `text-terracotta-700`), `.card`.
- **Text colour**: `stone-500` secondary, `stone-400` muted and every functional chevron (Disclosure, tile cues, row arrows), `stone-300` dividers only. `on-accent` was the ink on the terracotta tap; nothing outside `app/workbench` uses it since #459.
- **Dark**: ground #121212, surface #1c1c1c, raised #2a2a2a, stage #0a0a0a (the `.card-stage` panel steps up to surface), ink #f2f2f2. A `dark:` variant follows `data-theme` (#336), so pages style dark values with utilities. **The OS is the default** (#286): System, Light, Dark, `system` resolved identically by the boot script and `applyTheme`, `color-scheme` on the resolved theme. **Increase Contrast** (#448, `prefers-contrast: more`) restates the neutrals in both themes, the same ink stronger: hairlines 8% to 30%, edges to 50%, muted text to about 8:1; no hue changes.
- **Motion**: since #321 anything a finger or a layout moves runs on a spring (`lib/spring.ts`, damping and response) reaching CSS as `--spring-base` (0.35s), `--spring-snappy` (0.24s), `--spring-sheet` (0.42s), `--spring-thrown` (0.82, 0.36s), each with a `-duration`. Drags track 1:1, rubber-band, read velocity from the last 100ms and project the throw. A running spring hands its speed to the next one (#388). Every sheet (`Overlay`, `lib/sheet-gesture.ts`) holds a finger from its first downward move at any angle, from anywhere on it that is not mid-scroll (only the paywall's slider keeps sideways moves), and closes when thrown past half its height or pulled three quarters of the way to the screen's edge; a sheet once held never replays its rise (#394). Fades 200ms ease-out, 600 for celebration, the spin 2.2s (`SPIN`, #306). Durations come from tokens (`dur-*`, `--duration-*`), never inline (`lib/motion.test.ts`). Classes `.arrive`, `.arrive-x`, `.reveal`, `.fill`, `.star-land`, `.sheet-panel`, `.sheet-scrim`, `.rec-ring`. Reduced motion is `data-motion="reduce"`. A skeleton holds its height from the first frame and draws only after `--skeleton-wait` (300ms, #434), so a read that lands inside it never flashes one. `.press` scales 0.97 (0.985 on cards) and veils; `.press-row` rows light instead.
- **Rhythm**, owned by the parent (#333): the first block `mt-5` under a large title on every tab, section `mt-7`, head to content `mt-3`, row `py-3`, card to card `gap-3`, card `p-4`, hero `p-5`, screen `px-5` with `pb-[var(--nav-clear)]` (`--nav-h`, the 64px dock, plus its safe-area stand-off and 28px) on tab screens; `viewportFit: cover`. No empty band over 64px: a one-thing screen gives its free height to its stage, its art or its drop zone, never to ground (#368). Shared components carry no outer margin. The one tap is exactly 48px (`ACTION_CLASS`), bottom-anchored; flows and the one-thing screens (the lesson page, the trait page, the unit intro, Hostile, Calibrate) put it in `FooterShelf`.
- **Elevation**: `--shadow-1/2/3`, `.elev-1/2/3` (one `elev-2` per screen, `elev-3` floating).
- **Lists**: the iOS grouped list is `.inset-group` with `.group-row` (`:is(.flex)`, `:is(.grid)`), `.group-head`, `.group-foot`, `.switch`; focus is drawn inside the group's clip.
