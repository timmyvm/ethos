Read this instead of `DECISIONS.md`. When the system changes, update this file; the log keeps the history.

## Visual system

One system, since #234: Instrument's structure carrying Organic's warmth. The design pass (#326 to #355) put every screen on it and wrote its ten principles into DESIGN.md's "What this pass enforces"; `docs/look/audit/` holds that pass's before and after for every screen, both themes.

- **The ground is plain since #289**, Timothy's call. Light: ground #ffffff, surface #f4f4f4 (controls, tiles, inputs, the introduction's answer rows), raised #ffffff (cards, told apart by shadow and hairline). Dark: #121212, #1c1c1c, #262626. Shadows are neutral black in both themes; the accents and the ink stay warm.
- **One card, one lifted thing** (#328). `.card` is the only card recipe: raised fill, 1px `card-edge`, radius 16, shadow-1, and in dark a sheen (raised plus 4.6% white over the top 88px) because a black shadow vanishes on #121212; a card that takes another fill adds `bg-none`. Each screen has at most one `elev-2`, the card holding its one tap (Today's floor card, Practice's stage, Lessons' Up next). No card inside a card, no filled tile inside a card. Role materials stay their own: `.card-score` (deep sage, shadow-1 since #328), `.card-stage` (the stage, on the surface step in dark), `.card-premium` (plum), `.inset-group`, and Today's tone tiles.
- **Border jobs.** `card-edge` (.09/.12) is a card's hairline; `edge` (.14/.16) is a rule, a connector, an input's boundary, and the answer rows' 2px edge and 4px lip; `hairline` (.08) separates list rows.
- **Shapes.** Radius control 12, card 16, sheet 20 (the paywall, Practice's stage and /boss's headliner). Chips are the only pills, Record the only circle. Bars are square, trough (`sand`, #e4e4e4 light, #333333 dark) and fill.
- **Colour.** Terracotta #C67139 is the one tap per screen, with no hover step (#327): `.press` veils it on every pointer, terracotta-600 is only the recording state's Stop fill, and a disabled tap is `DISABLED_CLASS` (surface, edge, stone-400). Sage is earned (stars, streaks, points, the dimension bars). Plum #62336c is paid and appears only as `PremiumMark` and the two Premium surfaces: Practice's frosted wall and the plum paywall sheet (#343). Warm near-black is the stage.
- **Picture colour** since #300 and #311 to #315: ground, wash and label only, never a tap, never earned, never paid. **Trait tones** (`[data-trait]` → `--tone`, `--tone-wash`, `--tone-ink`, `--tone-stage`; Pausing blue, Fillers pink, Restarts amber, Pace green, Variety violet) go wherever a trait is shown, its ring, swatch, chip or name in its tone and its `content/traits.ts` name: Today, Lessons, the lesson and trait pages, results, Log, You (the lexicon too, in Variety's tone since #344), the introduction's pain glyphs. `lib/lesson-up-next.test.ts` and `lib/today-colour.test.ts` hold their contrast. The **introduction's tones** (`--pop-*`: sun, sky, mint, coral) colour stages and game tiles (Practice's sky stage, You's sky coin, freezes); Demos never stands on coral. The recording flow's warm ground is an amber wash.
- **Capitals are for data** (#329). Tracked caps (`.label-data`, `.label-micro`) only on data column heads and stat labels, at most three per viewport, never a ` · ` compound. Words over a title inside a card are sentence-case `.eyebrow`; the tab bar has no labels.

## Tokens (`app/globals.css`, `lib/motion.ts`, `lib/ui.ts`)

- **Type** (#331). Outfit 600/700/800 for numbers and UI, Figtree for body and Demos's voice. Text: `title` 26/700 (-0.02em, #332), `detail` 17/700, `read` 16 (reading paragraphs only), `body` 15, `row` 14/700, `link` 13/600, `caption` 12.5, `suffix` 10/700; large titles 34/800 and `.section-head` 20/800 come from their components. Numbers at 800 tabular: `num-xl` 72, `num-hero` 56, `num-l` 36, `num-m` 24, `num-s` 17. `lib/type-scale.test.ts` counts arbitrary `text-[Npx]` and `text-[Nrem]` sizes and may only go down (1, the splash wordmark). Headings balance, paragraphs wrap pretty, body sets `text-rendering: geometricPrecision`.
- **Components layer** (`@layer components`, so a colour utility beats it without `!`): `.eyebrow` (Outfit 13/600 stone-500, sentence case), `.detail-head` (17/700, a head one step in), `.section-head-count` (15/700 stone-400 after a section title), `.text-link` (13/600 stone-500, a 44px hit area from its own `::after`; the door you came for adds `text-terracotta-700`), `.card`.
- **Text colour**: `stone-500` secondary, `stone-400` muted and every functional chevron (Disclosure, tile cues, row arrows), `stone-300` dividers only. `on-accent` is ink on terracotta.
- **Dark**: ground #121212, surface #1c1c1c, raised #262626, stage #0a0a0a (the `.card-stage` panel steps up to surface), ink #f2f2f2. A `dark:` variant follows `data-theme` (#336), so pages style dark values with utilities. **The OS is the default** (#286): System, Light, Dark, `system` resolved identically by the boot script and `applyTheme`, `color-scheme` on the resolved theme.
- **Motion**: since #321 anything a finger or a layout moves runs on a spring (`lib/spring.ts`, damping and response) reaching CSS as `--spring-base` (0.35s), `--spring-snappy` (0.24s), `--spring-sheet` (0.42s), `--spring-thrown` (0.82, 0.36s), each with a `-duration`. Drags track 1:1, rubber-band, read velocity from the last 100ms and project the throw. Fades 200ms ease-out, 600 for celebration, the spin 2.2s (`SPIN`, #306). Durations come from tokens (`dur-*`, `--duration-*`), never inline (`lib/motion.test.ts`). Classes `.arrive`, `.arrive-x`, `.reveal`, `.fill`, `.star-land`, `.sheet-panel`, `.sheet-scrim`, `.rec-ring`. Reduced motion is `data-motion="reduce"`. `.press` scales 0.97 (0.985 on cards) and veils; `.press-row` rows light instead.
- **Rhythm**, owned by the parent (#333): the first block `mt-5` under a large title on every tab, section `mt-7`, head to content `mt-3`, row `py-3`, card to card `gap-3`, card `p-4`, hero `p-5`, screen `px-5` with `pb-[var(--nav-clear)]` (the 62px dock, its safe-area stand-off and 28px) on tab screens; `viewportFit: cover`. No empty band over 64px. Shared components carry no outer margin. The one tap is exactly 48px (`ACTION_CLASS`), bottom-anchored; flows put it in `FooterShelf`.
- **Elevation**: `--shadow-1/2/3`, `.elev-1/2/3` (one `elev-2` per screen, `elev-3` floating).
- **Lists**: the iOS grouped list is `.inset-group` with `.group-row` (`:is(.flex)`, `:is(.grid)`), `.group-head`, `.group-foot`, `.switch`; focus is drawn inside the group's clip.

## Data

`lib/client-data.ts`: every fetcher throws on a failed read, `fetchProfile` included, so a failed plan read is unknown, never free (#353); `fetchReps` reads the newest N and returns them oldest first. Unknown is never drawn as zero: a skeleton or a dash while loading or failed, `.arrive` when it lands.

## The daily challenge (#281)

`lib/challenge.ts`. One line a day, in the weakest trait's own unit, at the 30th percentile of the user's own trailing seven days by nearest rank: a number they already clear on about seven of every ten recordings. Frozen inside a week, absent under three readings, reading nothing from the outcome channel (a test enforces it). The prize is one coin for a week with five closed days (migration 0011). Not taken from `docs/closure.md`, inventory in #282: resumption, the comeback grant, the missed-day repair, the implementation intention, rest mode.

## Lessons and the path (#269)

**Lessons** are fifteen, three per trait, three practices each, chosen by the user; the last practice carries the free `no-notes` mod (#14). **The path** is `content/path.ts`, 120 practices the daily card draws from through `lib/next-practice.ts`, ranked on the RAW measurement; the hidden tier is never rendered or named. Lesson progress is derived from `lesson_id` on recordings (`lesson:<id>:<n>`, `lib/lesson-progress.ts`). A lesson practice's debrief offers the next practice of the same lesson (#273). The road is demoted, not retired: `UNITS`/`DRILLS` still back the debrief fallback, the star map and six test files (#272).
