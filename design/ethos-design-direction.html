03

Signature element: the pause bar

Every audio product draws sound and hides silence. Ethos inverts it. In our waveforms, held pauses render as solid amber — the gaps are the achievement. This is the product's core insight (silence, scored as a skill) made visible, and no competitor has it.

Rep 047 — playback

Stone bars are speech. Amber blocks are pauses you held on purpose. The longer you hold, the brighter it earns.

04

Layout scheme — three directions

Schematics, not screens. Terracotta block = the one CTA. Amber = streak/pause moments. Gray = everything that stays quiet.

A · The Floor — recommended

One thing on stage

Today's rep is a single dominant card mid-screen — you're handed the floor the moment you open the app. Streak and history recede below. Matches "one orange per screen" perfectly and makes the daily loop unmissable. Best for habit.

B · Training log

The feed of reps

Chronological log, every rep a row with its numbers — feels like a lifting app's history. Strong for progress-obsessives, but the daily action competes with the scroll.

C · Coach rail

Demos alongside

Persistent mascot rail with coach lines; content on the right. Warmest and most character-led, but risks classroom energy and eats mobile width. Save Demos for moments, not furniture.

06

Rules of the room

Always

  • One terracotta action per screen
  • Numbers in Space Grotesk, set big, hero position
  • Amber only for earned things: streaks, stars, held pauses
  • Warm neutrals — stone and cream, never gray or pure black
  • Coach register: short sentences, specific numbers

Never

  • Orange as decoration or background wash
  • Gradients, glassmorphism, purple anything
  • Sad mascot states or guilt notifications
  • Hype copy — "unlock", "transform", "potential"
  • Demos as permanent furniture — he appears at moments
