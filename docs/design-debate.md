# The design debate, 30 October

You asked for a debate on the design and then for the designs to change from it. A critic argued against all 55 design choices (D01 to D55) and an advocate argued for every one, both working from fresh shots of every screen. Three judges (user, craft and house) ruled on each choice, a planner merged the rulings into 15 items, and three builders built them. This page is the record.

## The headline

The debate kept the system and fixed the screens. 9 choices stand as built, 44 are refined, and 2 change outright: every 48px tap becomes a pill, and Practice's frosted Premium wall becomes a plain card listing the three modes. None of your own calls is reversed.

The build led with what a new user meets first:

- a gate that fails any empty band over 64px
- the trait page leading with the measured 1.8 instead of "18 PERCENTILE"
- answers picked in ink, so orange only ever means the next button
- the paywall's renewal terms moved above its button
- the recording screen keeping the day's trait colour instead of switching to amber
- Today's Start clearing 3:1 against its card on every trait day

One question is waiting for you (D21, below).

## The scoreboard

55 choices: 9 kept (D02, D07, D27, D34, D35, D41, D42, D43, D48), 44 refined, 2 changed (D11, D31). Counting the side that at least two of three judges named, the critic won 23 items, the advocate won 14, and 18 were split.

**The critic won the screens:** empty bands (D19), too many capitals (D10), the trait page leading with a percentile (D29), terracotta used for picking an answer (D47), the blue to amber break when a recording starts (D50), renewal terms hidden under the paywall's shelf (D40), the blurred Premium wall (D31), four different ways to close a sheet (D15), and Demos celebrating a +3 and a billing event (D45, D52).

**The advocate won the system:** the plain ground, terracotta with ink text and the one-tap rule; the sky stage on four tabs; direction B whole (the card, the rising object, the circles, the glows, the bento); the ladder, the Stair and the Go buttons; Demos on the stages and the listening Demos.

**Two of the critic's measurements fell.** Start's 1.32:1 was taken at the wrong row. The nine dimension denominators sum to 1000, so the scores do sum to the Index.

**One finding came from the judges, not the debate.** On the old colours, Start sat at about 2.1 to 3.0:1 against Today's card at its own row across the five trait days. S05 fixes it.

## The best lines

From the critic:

- "Ethos has a rulebook that is stronger than its screens."
- "The product says the numbers are the product, yet the loudest pixels are decoration."
- On the trait page: "For someone in the bottom fifth, that is the gap shown as a grade."
- On the introduction: "Two picks plus Next put three terracotta objects on one screen, and terracotta stops meaning tap."

From the advocate:

- "Terracotta means tap, sage means earned, plum means paid and stone holds the room, on a plain white or #121212 ground."
- "In each case the rule is right and the screen is short of it. The fix is to bring those screens up to the rule, not to drop the rule."
- On Stop: "Matching Stop's text to Record's would put a failing label on the control a speaker hits mid-sentence."
- On the listening Demos: "A listener in the speaker's eyeline is the practice itself, speaking to someone rather than to a screen."

## What changed: the 15 items

1. **S01, pills.** Every main button is a pill: Practice's Start, Carry on, Next, The numbers, the Log's Go and every 44 to 48px secondary button. Inputs and answer rows keep their 12px corners, and Record is still the only circle.
2. **S02, paywall.** The renewal terms sit in two lines just above the button, the button reads "Start at A$12.99 a month", and the stale "Games" line under the PREMIUM mark is gone.
3. **S03, trait page.** The ring leads with your measured number (1.8, its unit, then "18th percentile") instead of a grade, and the unit intro's title leads its page at 26/700.
4. **S04, introduction answers.** A picked answer is drawn in ink with a check, so Next is the only orange on the screen, and "Sharper at work or class" has a bulb instead of the spark.
5. **S05, colour.** Today's card reaches its dark foot above Start, so Start measures 3.2 to 3.9:1 on every trait day (it was 2.1 to 2.7). Restarts' orange is a readable #b06f00, dark mode gets a real raised step and a stage colour per trait, and deleting is a proper red.
6. **S06, gold and sage.** Stars and the lit flame are one gold everywhere, earned bars are a deeper sage that reads on sand, and a new account's chips read as unlit zeros.
7. **S07, empty bands.** A new gate fails any screen with an empty band over 64px. The score after a recording now fills its screen: stars, the Index, then Demos speaking under it. He celebrates only a new best or three stars.
8. **S08, recording screen.** Pressing Start keeps the day's colour, because the recording card is Today's card in the trait's family. Demos leaves the idle screen and arrives as the listener, and the live screen has a full-width wave in the trait's colour with an ink ring around Stop.
9. **S09, Log.** The score shows once, the hill is dated (4 Oct to 29 Oct), What moved drops its Index row, and each recording is a plain day-over-month date beside a one-line name.
10. **S10, tabs and backs.** The tab bar lights You on the Shop and Settings, Practice's tab is a microphone instead of a die, and every back link names where it goes.
11. **S11, Practice's Premium.** One plain card names Speed rush, Interview and Hostile Q&A with one Unlock. The frosted wall is gone, the free tiles are neutral, and the boss stage says "Read for 4 minutes. Explain it in 90 seconds."
12. **S12, You.** Day 1 vs now sits right under your three numbers (Streak, Best streak, XP this week), the road gets its own card, and the four locked traits fold into one row.
13. **S13, introduction.** The plan screen previews day one, its discs are plain numbered steps, Google sign-in uses Google's own button, the dark first intro screen is sky instead of mustard, and intro 2 says "Sixty seconds a day. Every one of them is measured." in place of the $5,000 line.
14. **S14, Today.** Each trait's object shows once, today's line shows once (in the wide tile and as a tick on its bar), nothing is cropped at the top at any width, and "Take this one" works while the topic is still spinning.
15. **S15, sheets.** Every information sheet closes with Done at the top right, the trait sheet shows your last number with a "Practise Pace" button, Cancel plan is red, and the blur behind a sheet drops from 6px to 2px.

When the three builds were merged, the sign-in page's Google button was brought in line with the introduction's, so both now use Google's own button.

## Your one question: D21

You picked direction B, "the object", after saying of the five reference homes: "They have beautiful home pages. we have a card with a panda. whats up with that." The B shot you chose puts Start as a pill beside two glass circles for spin and difficulty.

The critic's last unanswered point: #361 removed exactly these two controls because they "held the one tap to about 60% of the card's width", and on Today now Start is again about 64% of the card's width. All three judges kept the circles 3-0, because they are in the B you picked and the terracotta pill is still the only warm thing on the card.

**The question:** keep the circles as they are, or look at one squint strip of Today in both themes that compares them with #361's layout (bare 20px spin and difficulty glyphs on the eyebrow row, with Start at the card's full width)? Nothing changes unless you pick the glyphs. Until then the circles stay exactly as they are, and S05's darker card feet ship either way.

## How it was checked

The three builds were merged in order (A, then B, then C). The only text conflict was the trait page's frame, which kept both the capped stage and the named back. The merged tree passes the type check and every unit test (108 files, 2684 tests). Every tab and the touched flows were shot in light after the merge and looked at.

On a production build of the merge, every browser gate passes: no band over 64px on 76 screens, the Apple checks (28, including the new capitals count), Today's colour (22) and layout (15), the introduction (81), lessons (20), motion (25 and 12) and the Google return (45). The sheet touch gate passes 41 of 41 once its paywall caption selector follows the terms into the button's shelf. That gate belongs to another run, so the one-line fix waits for it.
