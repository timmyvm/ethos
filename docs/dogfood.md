# Dogfood log — the step-1 QA

vision.md: "If the loop doesn't hold Timothy for 14 straight days, nothing
ships wider." BUILD-PLAN definition of done: record a real rep on the
phone and the numbers match what you'd count by hand from the transcript.

## How to verify a rep by hand

1. Do the rep. Open the transcript on the results screen.
2. Count fillers yourself: um, uh, like (as filler, not comparison),
   you know, sort of, kind of, basically, actually, literally.
3. Count words ÷ minutes for WPM.
4. Replay the audio, count silences ≥ ~1 beat ("one-mississippi" ≈ 0.8s).
5. Log below. Anything off by more than ±1 filler or ±10 WPM is an
   engine bug — file it with the transcript.

## What to watch beyond the numbers

- Did the whole loop stay under 5 minutes, including the wait for scoring?
- Did the focus feel like coaching or like a horoscope? (If it names no
  number, it's a bug — the validator should have caught it.)
- Did the supply quote your actual words? Was the upgrade one you'd use?
- Day the streak urge kicked in (that's the step-3 signal).

Step 3+ stays frozen until this table is full and the numbers match.
