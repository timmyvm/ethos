# 05 — Conversion Copywriter: Hero, Cap Moment, Pushes, Pricing Page

**Role:** Conversion copywriter (direct-response: Schwartz stages, Ogilvy specificity, Copyhackers VoC method) · **Date:** 2026-08-12
**Mandate:** The words for four surfaces — landing hero, the daily-cap paywall moment, five pushes, the pricing page — each built to move exactly one metric.

---

## 0. Operating stance

- **Method, every asset:** (1) name the reader's awareness stage; (2) one big
  idea per screen; (3) specificity over cleverness — but never a fabricated
  outcome statistic: we have zero users, so every number in copy is a number
  the *product measurably does* (60 seconds, /1000, 1 deep read a day),
  never a result we don't have ("cut fillers 40% in a week" is banned until
  a real cohort proves it); (4) the reader's words for the problem, all
  marked **UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS** because no user quotes exist; (5) every
  line's only job is earning the next line.
- **Voice:** Demos — warm, wry, direct. Coach register (brand.md): short
  sentences, specific numbers, zero hype adjectives. The read-aloud test was
  applied to every line below: if Demos couldn't say it out loud to your
  face, it didn't ship. This is a speaking app; clunky copy is a product
  contradiction.
- **Banned and checked against every line:** confidence (#87 — the dimension
  displays as "Steadiness"), alpha, dominate, high-value, top 1%, level up
  your life, unlock your potential (and "unlock" generally in marketing
  copy), take-X-to-the-next-level, revolutionize, game-changer, "elevate
  your", feature lists disguised as benefits, manufactured insecurity of any
  kind. Counters say "recordings"; "rep" stays the verb (#106).
- **Colleague contracts adopted:** 01's metric tree (every asset names its
  tree node), 02's event vocabulary (no parallel names) and their §4 item 4
  (VoC lines stay UNVALIDATED until a behavioural count exists), 04's
  framing directions for the paywall (adopted with two refinements, §3.5),
  03's Callback line style (§7).
- Nothing here ships tonight. Post-flip surfaces (§3, parts of §5) are
  design-for-the-flip per #96 — the copy exists so flip day doesn't write
  copy in a hurry.
- **ASSUMPTION (intake unfilled): ~15 focused founder-hours/week.**
  **ASSUMPTION (intake unfilled): soft launch / public beta within ~4–6
  weeks** — the hero test (§2.4) is scoped to that window.
- Market: AU-first; all prices AUD per 04 §5.2's candidate B placeholder.
- One formal challenge raised (§8): where the two locked #86 headlines
  collide on the landing hero.

## 1. Voice-of-customer bank — every line UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS

The reader's words, not ours. Drafted from the persona (vision.md: 16–28,
self-improvement-driven, self-diagnoses the gap) and the Wellspoken
teardown's symptom framing (mechanics.md — structure adopted, their users'
words are not our users' words). **Every row is an UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS
until a real user says it; replace this table with real quotes from hello@
replies (#83) and observed sessions as they arrive.**

| Their words (hypothesised) | The felt moment | Product truth it maps to |
|---|---|---|
| "I ramble" | The point existed, the sentence didn't end | Structure score, WPM, repeated n-grams |
| "I go blank" | Silence that feels like panic, not poise | Pause map: hesitation vs landed pauses (#97) |
| "I say 'like' constantly and hear it as I do it" | Self-caught, mid-sentence, can't stop | Filler count + timestamps |
| "People talk over me" | Trailing off invites the interruption | Endings that land (Structure), pace |
| "I replay conversations afterwards" | The better sentence arrives an hour late | Daily reps: the next one is tomorrow, trained |
| "I sound smarter in my head" | The gap between who they are and how they sound (vision.md, verbatim) | The whole product |
| "I don't have a speech coming up, it just bothers me" | Trait, not deadline | Gym positioning, not prep |

Awareness note: **we never write for the unaware stage.** vision.md: the
persona self-diagnoses — "we never have to convince them the problem
exists." Budgeting words to convince the unconvinced is spend against a
reader we didn't choose. The ladder for Ethos runs problem-aware →
solution-aware → product-aware, and §2 writes one hero for each.

## 2. Landing page hero — 3 variants + the 5 symptom-first candidates

Positioning per the DECISIONS open queue: these are **test candidates
alongside the locked #86 pair**, not replacements — except where §8's
formal challenge proposes a placement change, which is Timothy's call.

**Shared metric (01 tree, branch A):** landing view → rep-1 started.
Event chain per 02: `welcome_viewed{screen:1}` → `rep_started{rep_number:1}`.
The hero has exactly one job: get the visitor to the mic. Not signups (#15
— there's nothing to sign up for yet), not scroll depth, not brand recall.

### 2.1 Variant A — problem-aware (default for cold traffic)

> **You don't ramble because you have nothing to say.**
> Ethos is a daily speech gym. One 60-second rep into your mic, hard
> numbers on your fillers, pace and pauses — and one fix for tomorrow.
> Five minutes. No audience.
>
> CTA: **Take the floor — it's free**

- **Stage:** problem-aware. They know the symptom; they don't know reps
  exist as a category.
- **One big idea:** the gap is a skill gap, not a character flaw — and
  skills take reps.
- **Rationale:** the headline names the symptom in their words ("I ramble",
  UNVALIDATED) while doing the one thing manufactured-insecurity copy never
  does: siding with the reader. It asserts they have something to say —
  the dignity is the hook, and it is also vision.md compliance by
  construction. Subhead moves them one stage: mechanism (reps + numbers),
  cost (60 seconds), objection kill (no audience).
- **Objection it overcomes:** "this is just who I am." No — it's a
  measurable habit, and measurable things train.

### 2.2 Variant B — solution-aware (comparison/search traffic)

> **Courses teach theory. Coaches cost $150 an hour. This is reps.**
> 60 seconds a day into your mic. Ethos counts your fillers, maps your
> pauses — including whether your silences land or panic — and scores you
> out of 1000. Then it gives you exactly one thing to fix.
>
> CTA: **Do your first rep**

- **Stage:** solution-aware. They've seen courses, coaches, maybe an app.
  The headline positions on **format, not features** (#85 — Yoodli is
  never named, nothing false or checkable-against-us is claimed).
- **One big idea:** everyone else sells knowledge or rehearsal; Ethos sells
  daily reps.
- **Rationale:** vision.md's own problem statement ("Courses teach theory.
  Coaches cost $150/hr. Nobody offers reps.") is the best solution-aware
  copy in the repo — it was written as positioning and works as a headline
  almost verbatim. The subhead spends its specificity on the one claim no
  competitor makes: silence scored as a skill.
- **Objection it overcomes:** "another speaking course I'll abandon by
  week 2." A rep is 60 seconds; a course is a guilt object.

### 2.3 Variant C — product-aware (heard of Ethos; return visitors, link-in-bio)

> **Your first rep takes 60 seconds. No account, no audience.**
> Record once. Get your filler count, your pace, your pause map, and your
> first Ethos Index out of 1000. Then decide.
>
> CTA: **Take the floor**

- **Stage:** product-aware. They know what Ethos is; they haven't tried it.
  The headline is the *offer*, and the offer is friction removal.
- **One big idea:** trying costs nothing — not money, not identity, not an
  email address.
- **Rationale:** anonymous-first (#15, #133) is a real structural
  advantage; most-aware readers convert on the deal, not the dream
  (Schwartz). "Then decide." hands control back — the exact opposite of the
  quiz-wall the category is resented for (mechanics.md).
- **Objection it overcomes:** "I don't want anyone — including an app — to
  have my voice before I've decided." Nothing is asked for before value.

### 2.4 The 5 symptom-first candidates (closes the open-queue item)

The DECISIONS open queue asks for 5 symptom-first landing headlines to test
on the persona. All built on §1's **UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS** rows; all name
a self-diagnosed felt moment without manufacturing it:

1. **You don't ramble because you have nothing to say.** (= Variant A)
2. **The point was clear in your head. Then you opened your mouth.**
3. **You replay the conversation an hour later, with better lines.**
4. **"Like" nine times a minute is a habit, not a personality.**
5. **You go quiet in rooms where you know the answer.**

Test protocol (pre-data, founder-runnable inside the ~4–6 week window):
show each of the 7 lines (these 5 + both locked #86 lines) to 5 people in
the persona for 5 seconds each; ask "what does this product do?" and
"would you tap?". Clarity first, pull second. Post-gate (01 §4.3), the real
test is sequential-cohort landing→rep-1-start — never a 50-user A/B (02 §4
item 1). **WAITING ON DATA — the winner is not chosen tonight.**

### 2.5 Where the locked lines sit (per #86, unchanged tonight)

"Practice being worth listening to." holds acquisition surfaces (store
copy, link previews, ads-if-ever); "Earn the room." holds hero, onboarding,
merch. Both survive the read-aloud test easily — the challenge in §8 is
about *placement on one surface*, not quality.

## 3. Paywall copy — the daily-cap moment (2nd deep read, #75)

**Everything in this section is post-flip copy (#96). None of it renders in
the free era — including any meter UI (03 §3.2's rule, adopted by 04).**

**Stage:** most-aware — this reader is *using the product right now* and
just did their 2nd recording of the day. No persuasion about what Ethos is;
only about what Pro continues.
**Metric:** `paywall_viewed{surface: judged_meter}` → `upgrade_started`
(02's dormant events), guardrailed by exposed-cohort W2R (02 §3, paywall
row — a conversion lift with a W2R sag is a loss).
**Objection to overcome:** "I hit a wall, so the free thing was bait."
Every line below exists to make that sentence untrue and *feel* untrue.

### 3.1 The cap card (in the results walk, where the judged tier would render)

The rep above this card is fully delivered — Index-eligible measured
metrics, transcript, pause bar, stars — per #75/#76. The card:

> **Today's deep read went to your 9:14am rep.**
> The next one lands at midnight. Banked: ●●○ 2 ready.
> Everything measured on this rep is above — that never runs out.
>
> Quiet text link: *Keep today's coaching going → Ethos Pro*

Rules embodied: banked reads render as held objects, never debts (04
§1.1); the card names the user's own timestamp (no-horoscope discipline
applied to commerce); the third line kills the bait objection in place;
zero-banked state simply drops the "Banked" line — never "0 banked", which
reads as an empty wallet. No terracotta on this card — the one terracotta
tap on a results screen belongs to the walk's forward action, not a sell.

### 3.2 The sheet — loss framing (primary, per 04's direction)

> Reason line: *2 recordings today. 1 deep read.*
> **Keep today's coaching going.**
> Pro reads every rep the way it read your 9:14 — scores with quoted
> moments, one focus, one word worth stealing from yourself.
>
> - **Deep reads on every rep** — Structure, Credibility, Engagement,
>   Steadiness, each citing a moment from your transcript. Free: 1 a day.
> - **Presence on video reps** — the score, the timestamped moments, the
>   week-over-week line.
> - **Your whole history** — every recording, day 1 vs day N, every
>   dimension's trendline.
> - **Your whole lexicon** — every swap you've earned, ready to drill.
>   *(bullet held until 04's Challenge M1 is ruled — if #31 stands as
>   written, this line becomes "Your lexicon archive — every swap beyond
>   the latest 3.")*
> - **The boss library** — any Cold Topic, any time.
>
> Annual card (selected): **A$6.67 a month** · billed A$79.99 today · save 55%
> Monthly card: A$14.99 a month
>
> CTA (terracotta): **Start Pro**
> Decline (plain text): *Not yet*
> Footer: **Money never buys stars, streaks, or scores.** · Cancel anytime · Restore purchase

Notes: the bullet list promises only what is built — Debate and Hostile
Q&A do not appear on a payment sheet until they exist (a paywall is the one
surface where "coming soon" is taking money for vapor). "Steadiness" per
#87. The footer's first line stays verbatim (04 §4.2.7 — the most
differentiated sentence on the screen).

### 3.3 The sheet — gain framing (E1's test variant, written now so the test is real)

> Reason line: *2 recordings today. 1 deep read.*
> **A deep read on every rep.**
> You recorded twice today. Pro would have read both — and every rep after.

Body, plans, CTA, footer identical to §3.2. The E1 experiment (04 §6)
varies exactly one thing — the headline+sub pair — so a result means
something. **WAITING ON DATA — do not run pre-flip or pre-gate.**

### 3.4 The other entry surfaces — headlines finalized (04 §4.2.2 asked role 5 for the words)

| Surface | Headline (final copy) | Framing |
|---|---|---|
| Day-3 proactive (after the card, once) | **Three days of numbers. Keep all of them.** | Loss — honest: the numbers exist and are theirs |
| Archive (day-8+ log scroll) | **Your first rep is still here.** | Loss as possession — held, never deleted |
| Presence readout (post video rep) | **See what the camera measured.** | Gain — nothing was taken; curiosity is the sale |
| Boss library (post weekly win) | **Good. Want another?** | Gain — appetite after a win, in Demos's actual voice |

04's first three directions adopted verbatim — they were already the best
version. The boss line is mine: "any topic, any time" is a bullet, not a
headline; the headline's job is to catch the "another one" impulse mid-air.

### 3.5 Where I diverge from 04's framing (stated, not silently substituted)

1. **CTA: "Start Pro", not "Start with annual."** Read both aloud. "Start
   with annual" answers "which billing plan?" — a question the selected
   plan card already answers — and makes the button restate the selector.
   The button should name the decision, not the invoice. Plan choice lives
   on the cards; the one terracotta tap says the one big idea. (If checkout
   requires plan-explicitness for AU consumer-law comfort, "Start Pro —
   A$79.99/yr" on the button is the compromise; still never "Subscribe",
   which names our mechanism instead of their action.)
2. **"Keep today's coaching going" needs its card, or it overclaims.** On
   rep 2, today's deep read already happened — nothing is mid-stream to
   "keep going" unless the surface says what continues. §3.1's card line
   ("The next one lands at midnight") is load-bearing: with it, the
   headline reads as continuity; without it, it edges toward the "you've
   run out" framing 04 itself bans. Adopted, with the dependency named.
   Both versions written (§3.2/§3.3) per the brief; my prior matches 04's
   — loss wins at this moment — and E1 settles it.

## 4. Five push notifications — Demos voice

Rules from mechanics.md + #26, restated as copy law: one notification per
day, maximum; quiet hours 10pm–7am; loss-aversion framing allowed, guilt
and sad-mascot states are not; coach register — numbers, not vibes. All
five compete for one daily slot, so precedence is part of the spec:
**streak-at-risk > new-boss > league demotion warning > league promotion >
lapsed** (lapsed and streak-at-risk are mutually exclusive by definition).
Metric for all five per 02: `reminder_clicked` → a rep row the same local
day. A push that gets opens but no reps is furniture (#42's honesty rule,
applied to copy).

### 4.1 Streak-at-risk (evening, before quiet hours)

> **12 days, still standing.**
> One 60-second rep before midnight makes it 13.

- Loss aversion on the number, zero guilt about the person. States the
  cost (60 seconds) and the deadline (midnight, #77's local day) — both
  facts, both numbers. No "don't lose it!", no sad Demos (#26).
- Objection overcome: "no time tonight." 60 seconds is not a time problem.

### 4.2 League promotion

> **Promoted.**
> Your reps topped the league. New room opens Monday — same job: show up
> and speak.

- One-word headline because the event is the message. "New room" quietly
  rhymes with "Earn the room." without spending the locked line. "Same
  job" resets expectation before the harder league inflates it.
- **Dormant until the league renders for anyone** — 01's Challenge 1
  (density gate) is pending and 02 cut league events; this copy ships to a
  drawer, not to production.

### 4.3 League demotion warning

> **Drop zone, for now.**
> The league closes Sunday night. Reps move you — nothing else does.

- The honesty constraint bit hard here: "one rep holds your place" is a
  promise about *other people's* behaviour we can't keep. So the copy
  states only what is true: the deadline, and that reps are the only lever
  (which is also the no-pay-to-win doctrine in eight words). "For now"
  carries the forward frame. Banned framings avoided by construction:
  "you're falling behind everyone" (mechanics.md, verbatim ban).
- Dormant with §4.2, same reason.

### 4.4 Lapsed 3 days

> **Day 13 is one rep away.**
> The day counter only counts up. 612 is still your number to beat.

- #51's law governs this one: fresh-start framing points forward ONLY —
  the miss is never mentioned, so this push never says "it's been 3 days"
  (that sentence is a guilt mechanic wearing a calendar). It leans
  entirely on #93's day counter — the number that never resets — and the
  user's own best Index. Both numbers are theirs; neither is a wound.
- Objection overcome: "I broke the streak, so the tally's ruined." The
  counter that can't break says otherwise.

### 4.5 New boss mode (weekly Cold Topic lands)

> **This week's Cold Topic just dropped.**
> 3 minutes to study something you've never met. 90 seconds to explain it.

- The appointment mechanic (03 §2's countdown-chip logic, notification
  half). The copy sells the *format* — the numbers 3 and 90 are the whole
  pitch — and "something you've never met" makes the difficulty the draw,
  not a threat. No topic name in the push: the reveal is the variable
  reward, and it happens in-app.
- Objection overcome: "boss = long = not today." It's under 5 minutes
  total, and the numbers say so.

## 5. Pricing page

**Stage:** product-aware to most-aware — nobody arrives at /pricing by
accident. The page's one big idea: **the free tier is the product, Pro is
depth on a habit you already have.** (04 §0.1, compressed to a sentence.)
**Metric:** pricing view → `upgrade_started`, guardrailed as ever by W2R.
**Objection to overcome:** "freemium means the free tier is crippleware."
The page leads with what never costs money, which no competitor's pricing
page dares to do — that IS the differentiation (chess.com model, #75).

### 5.1 Plan names

**Ethos** (free) and **Ethos Pro**. No cutesy tier names ("Starter",
"Champion"), no third decoy tier (04 §5.3, adopted). The free plan keeps
the product's name because it is the product; Pro is one honest syllable
that the persona already reads as "the paid one" from every app they own.

### 5.2 The plans, as the page states them

> **Ethos — free, forever.**
> The daily rep, every measured number, the pause map, the live ring,
> one deep read a day, the full 29-lesson path, streaks and coins.
> Not a trial. This is the gym.
>
> CTA: **Take the floor**

> **Ethos Pro.**
> A deep read on every rep. Your whole history, day 1 vs day N. The
> Presence readout on video reps. The boss library.
>
> **A$6.67 a month** — billed A$79.99 once a year · save 55%
> or A$14.99 monthly
>
> CTA (the page's one terracotta): **Start Pro**
> Under it, plain: *Cancel anytime. Money never buys stars, streaks, or scores.*

"Not a trial. This is the gym." is the crippleware objection answered in
seven words, and it is checkably true under #75/#141 (free path never
walled, measured tier unlimited). Prices are 04's candidate B —
**placeholder until the open-queue pricing pass closes; re-verify from an
AU device at flip (04 §5.1).**

### 5.3 The annual anchor line

> **A year of Pro costs about half of one hour with a speech coach.**

A$79.99 against vision.md's own stated alternative (~A$150/hr). Checkable
arithmetic, zero adjectives, and it reframes the annual commitment from
"12 months of app subscription" to "one purchase vs one hour" — the anchor
does the persuading so the copy never has to push. The per-month figure
(A$6.67) does the within-page anchoring against monthly (04 §5.3,
adopted); the honest total sits beside it, always.

### 5.4 CTA copy — the argument, since button copy is conversion copy

- **"Start Pro"** — chosen. Names the action and the identity of the thing;
  survives the read-aloud test; works on the sheet (§3.2) and the page
  identically, so the product speaks one language.
- "Subscribe" — rejected: names our billing mechanism, not their decision.
  It is the "Submit" of commerce.
- "Start speaking better" — rejected, narrowly: the outcome claim is the
  page's job, already done by the bullets; on the button it reads as a
  promise the button-press itself can't keep (pressing doesn't make you
  better; reps do — and this brand does not blur that line). Also fails
  the one-big-idea rule: the button would argue the product while the
  plan card argues the price.
- "Go annual" / "Start with annual" — rejected per §3.5.1: the button
  restating the plan selector is two surfaces doing one job badly.
- Free plan's **"Take the floor"** — matches the in-product verb (#133's
  welcome CTA and #9's floor metaphor), so the marketing site and the app
  make one promise in the same words.

### 5.5 What this page never says

No "trusted by thousands" (false at our N; fake social proof stays banned
at every N — 04, adopted). No countdown, no launch discount, no strike-
through pricing. No competitor names (#85: format, not feature war). No
"per day" price gymnastics ("less than a coffee!") — the anchor is a
coach's hour because that is the real alternative, not a beverage. And no
word from the banned list, which was checked line by line.

## 8. Formal challenge

### Challenge C1 — #86 puts the brand line on the one surface that needs the clarity line (amend placement, not the lines)

**Claim:** the landing hero of speakethos.com is simultaneously "hero"
(assigned "Earn the room.") and the product's single most important
*acquisition surface* (assigned "Practice being worth listening to.").
#86's two rules collide there, and its own rationale — "clarity converts
at zero awareness; the brand line translates the name once the category is
established" — argues for resolving the collision in favour of clarity:
at pre-launch, zero visitors have the category context "Earn the room."
requires. A cold, problem-aware visitor should meet a symptom-first or
clarity headline (§2.1/§2.4 candidates, or the locked clarity line);
"Earn the room." should close the page, own onboarding (where context now
exists), and own merch — placements where its compression is a strength.
**Evidence:** Schwartz's stage rule (the headline is dictated by awareness,
and cold traffic is at most problem-aware); #86's own two-line rationale,
quoted above; the DECISIONS open queue itself requesting 5 symptom-first
headlines "to test on the persona" — a request that only makes sense if
the hero slot is genuinely contestable.
**Test that settles it:** §2.4's protocol — persona five-second test on
all 7 lines now (clarity comprehension + tap intent), then post-gate
sequential cohorts on landing→rep-1-start (`welcome_viewed` →
`rep_started{rep_number:1}`), one headline per cohort. If "Earn the room."
matches the best challenger within noise, #86 stands untouched and this
challenge dies — the lines themselves are never in question, only which
one greets a stranger.
