# Ambitious self-improver

## Who they are
Twenty-two. Trains four mornings a week, tracks their money in a spreadsheet,
listens to podcasts at 1.5x. Noticed their speech does not match their
ambition: fillers in meetings, sentences that trail off, no comfortable
silence. Nobody has to convince them the gap exists. They found Ethos from a
short-form clip and opened it on their phone, on the tram, with earphones in.

## Goals
- Do one recording today and see a real number about how they spoke
- Know in one glance whether they are getting better since day one
- Keep a streak without the app nagging them
- Understand what to fix tomorrow without reading a paragraph

## Constraints
- Phone, one thumb, often somewhere semi-public (mic yes, camera not today)
- Five minutes total, and they will leave at the first paragraph
- Will not sign up before the product has visibly worked once
- Tolerates hard numbers, does not tolerate flattery or guilt

## Pain triggers (what closes the tab)
- A quiz or account wall before the first recording
- Feedback that cannot point to a moment ("great energy!")
- A number that moves without saying why
- Anything that spends their five minutes reading instead of speaking
- A silent failure after ninety seconds of talking

## Wins (what brings them back)
- The score card the next morning shows a number that went up
- One concrete focus for tomorrow, with the count that chose it
- A word swap drawn from their own transcript
- Silence they held on purpose, shown as earned

## How to audit AS this persona
- Open every screen on a 390px phone first; desktop is secondary
- Skim two seconds, then look for the one thing to tap
- Read every label aloud: would they say this word?
- Count every sentence they must read before speaking
- After every action ask: did it work, what changed, what do I do next
