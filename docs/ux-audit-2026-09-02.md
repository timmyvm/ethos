## Executive summary

The daily loop is honest and fast: a fresh browser gets three screens and a mic, records, waits on a real waveform, gets a number with a delta and a focus, and can leave through a save wall that keeps its promise. The states the design constitution demands mostly exist and were exercised: skeletons that end, error cards with retries, an outbox that survives a dead network, a keyboard path through the whole recording, a dark theme that repaints correctly. What fails is narrower and sharper: a viewport meta that disables zoom, the pause bar clipped on the persona's own device, a backend error that dresses up as "you have done nothing", contrast that sits below the floor the design rules set, and two navigation contradictions at the product's most important moments (which lesson is next, and where you land after paying). The North Star fix is H-2 plus H-1: make the numbers trustworthy on the day the network is bad, and let the signature element fit the phone.

## Method, and what this audit could not reach

The skill prefers the deployed app. From this sandbox, Chromium's TLS handshake to `speakethos.com` was dropped by the session's egress proxy (curl reaches it, the browser does not), and the sandbox holds no Supabase, OpenAI or Anthropic credentials. So the audit ran the **same commit locally** (`next build` + `next start`) against:

- a local **mock of the Supabase HTTP surface** the browser client uses (GoTrue anonymous sign-in, user, refresh, update, password sign-in, recover, OAuth bounce; PostgREST select/insert/upsert/delete/rpc with the filters `lib/client-data.ts` sends; Storage signed URLs serving a real WAV). Rows persist for the session, so round-trips and the seeded Day 0 / 1 / 7 / 30 / 95 horizons are real client reads through the real client code.
- a mock of **`POST /api/analyze`** that runs the product's own deterministic engine (`computeMetrics`, `pauseReport`, `tier1Scores`, `tier2Anchors`, `ethosIndex`) over synthetic word streams, and fabricates only the LLM layer (coach copy and judged dimensions), quoting the transcript verbatim as the product's citation rule demands. Every stored row has the shape `saveRep` writes. Failure modes (502, 429, network drop, slow, unstored) were injected here.
- a mock of **`POST /api/hostile`** (one quoted question per round, a cited verdict) and **`POST /api/redeem`** (`ETHOS-PRO` flips `profiles.premium`) so the boss and the premium role could be audited.

Everything about layout, copy, interaction, states, keyboard, a11y and the client's data handling is therefore the real code on the real build. **Out of reach and not claimed**: Whisper transcription quality, real engine latency, real email delivery, web push, the boss fact-check, Google OAuth end to end (the mock's redirect did not complete the link; not counted as a finding), and Presence scoring on a real body (the fake camera has no body in it). The delete-account route returned 401 locally because it needs the service-role key; that line is allowlisted as environment.

Evidence lives in `docs/ux-audit-2026-09-02/evidence/` (every screenshot cited below, the 390px sweep shots `p4-*-390.png`, the axe/perf report `p4-report.json`, the Phase 7 verify log `p7-verify.out`). Paths below are relative to it. The full session capture (638 manifest entries, per-session console/network JSON, phase outputs) stayed in the session scratchpad and is summarised in the tables, not committed.

## Findings

Format per finding: Layer · Severity · Surface · Persona · Reproduce · Observed · Expected · Evidence · Suspected location · Smallest possible patch. "Fixed" means fixed in this session and re-walked (Appendix B).

### Critical (hard gates)

#### [C-1] Pinch-zoom is disabled on every page

- **Layer**: Visual (a11y) · **Severity**: Critical (axe `meta-viewport`, WCAG 1.4.4) · **Surface**: all 20 routes · **Persona**: any low-vision reader; the product's content is numbers
- **Reproduce**: 1. Open any route. 2. Read `<meta name="viewport">`. 3. Try to pinch-zoom on a phone.
- **Observed**: `content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1"`; axe critical ×1 on all 20 routes at both widths.
- **Expected**: a user-scalable viewport.
- **Evidence**: `p4-report.json` → `axe["/@390"]` and every other route.
- **Suspected location**: `app/layout.tsx:69` `maximumScale: 1`.
- **Smallest possible patch**: delete that line. **Fixed.**

### High

#### [H-1] The pause bar overflows and is clipped at phone widths

- **Layer**: Visual · **Severity**: High (layout collapse at 375/390; the signature element, DECISIONS #8) · **Surface**: `/rep` debrief step 2, `/rep/[id]`, `/upload` result at ≤ 430px · **Persona**: every phone user
- **Reproduce**: 1. Record ~50–90s. 2. Open "The numbers". 3. Compare the bar's right edge with the timeline.
- **Observed**: the row `div.mt-3.flex.h-14.items-center.gap-[3px].overflow-hidden` has `scrollWidth 390` vs `clientWidth 318`; five tick spans sit at x 387–426, outside the 390px viewport; the last ~9s of a 52s recording are cut off.
- **Expected**: the bar fits its card at any width; every second of the recording is visible.
- **Evidence**: `t1-11-results-numbers.png`, layout probe `results numbers 390`.
- **Suspected location**: `components/PauseBar.tsx:35` (`tick = max(1.6, durationS / 34)` → up to 34 ticks × 8px) and `:61,83` (`shrink-0`, fixed pixel widths).
- **Smallest possible patch**: let ticks and pills shrink (`flex: 0 1 <basis>`, a small `min-w`) so the row always fits. **Fixed.**

#### [H-2] A backend error renders the zero state instead of the error state

- **Layer**: Architecture / Feedback · **Severity**: High (data integrity; the exact lie DECISIONS #147 forbids) · **Surface**: `/`, `/history`, `/you`, `/shop`, the debrief's "since last time" · **Persona**: anyone whose Supabase call answers 500, or whose JWT has expired (401)
- **Reproduce**: 1. Make PostgREST answer 500 (or let a JWT expire). 2. Load `/`.
- **Observed**: `Day one starts today.` on home, `0 recordings.` on the log, zeros on `/you`, 0 coins in the shop; no error, no retry. A read that **hangs** does end in the error card at 8.4s and the retry recovers the numbers.
- **Expected**: `Your score didn't load.` with a retry, on every screen that depends on the read (#147).
- **Evidence**: `p5b-read-fail-home.png`, `p5b-read-fail-history.png`, `p5b-read-hang-ended.png`, `p5b-read-recovered.png`.
- **Suspected location**: `lib/client-data.ts:56-76` `fetchReps` and `fetchRep` discard supabase-js's `error` and return `[]` / `null`; `lib/load.ts` `readable()` only catches throws.
- **Smallest possible patch**: `if (error) throw error;` in both. **Fixed.**

#### [H-3] Home and the debrief disagree about the next lesson

- **Layer**: Architecture / Interaction · **Severity**: High (the two primary taps point at different lessons) · **Surface**: `/rep` debrief exit vs `/` floor and road · **Persona**: day 1 onward
- **Reproduce**: 1. Finish The baseline with 1★. 2. Read the debrief's primary button. 3. Go home; read the floor and the road.
- **Observed**: debrief says `Next lesson · Kill 'like'` (`nextDrill`, rotation order); home says `Extra lesson · The baseline · Go again` and the road marks lesson 1 as `Today · same recording as the card`. With six lessons already starred (day-7 seed) home still pins to lesson 1 because `nextLesson` returns the first lesson under 3★.
- **Expected**: one answer to "what do I do next", on both surfaces.
- **Evidence**: `t1-13-results-words.png` vs `t1-20-home-after-rep1.png`; `t2-11-landed-next-lesson.png` vs `t2-12-home-after-rep.png`.
- **Suspected location**: `app/rep/page.tsx:1407` `nextDrill(config.lessonId)`; `lib/path.ts` `nextLesson` (first lesson `< 3` stars).
- **Smallest possible patch**: the debrief takes the path's answer (`nextLesson(starsByLesson(repsNow))`); when that is the lesson just done the button reads `Go again · <lesson>` and the duplicate `Retake this one` hides. **Fixed.** The underlying rule (pin the floor to a lesson until it has 3★) is tested and decided; whether it should advance on any star is Timothy's call and is logged in `docs/devibe/needs-judgment.md`.

#### [H-4] Redeeming a code from the day-3 moment or the cap line ends on an empty Record screen

- **Layer**: Interaction / Feedback · **Severity**: High (confusion at the conversion moment) · **Surface**: `/rep` (ProgressMoment → Paywall, capped debrief → Paywall) · **Persona**: day 3+, paying
- **Reproduce**: 1. Reach the day-3 progress moment. 2. `Keep every number` → `Start with annual` → `I have a code` → a valid code → `Unlock`. 3. Wait 900ms.
- **Observed**: `Unlocked. Premium is on this account now.` then `window.location.reload()` drops the debrief and lands on `/rep?lesson=f1` at phase idle: the lesson just finished, a Record button, no continuation.
- **Expected**: the exit continues to the tapped destination with premium on.
- **Evidence**: `t2b-10-code-after.png`, `t2c-05-unlocked-ack.png`, `t2c-06-after-redeem-landing.png`.
- **Suspected location**: `components/Paywall.tsx:93`.
- **Smallest possible patch**: `onUnlocked?: () => void` on Paywall (default reload); the debrief passes `() => setPremium(true)` for the cap sheet and the progress moment passes its exit. **Fixed.**

#### [H-5] The stone mid-tones and the terracotta tap fail the contrast floor the design rules set

- **Layer**: Visual (a11y) · **Severity**: High (axe serious on all 20 routes, both themes) · **Surface**: every screen · **Persona**: anyone outdoors on a phone; the captions carry the "why" behind every number
- **Reproduce**: 1. Open any route. 2. Run axe `color-contrast`, or measure with the eyedropper.
- **Observed (light)**: stone-300 `#a0988d` on ground `#f5ead8` **2.39:1** (future road rows, 21 nodes on home), month labels `#b5ada0` **1.86:1**, inactive nav tabs `#b9b3a8` on raised **1.88:1**, stone-400 `#958e84` **2.72:1** (every eyebrow label, every caption, the anon line), stone-500 `#807a71` **3.57:1** (the prompt under the lesson title, `← back`, the unit line), cream `#fdf6e7` on terracotta `#c67139` **3.35:1** (the one tap per screen, the Record button; 15px bold is not "large text"). **Dark**: stone on ground 3.41 / 3.99, nav 2.45, month labels 2.48, the CTA 3.35.
- **Expected**: ≥ 4.5:1 "against its surface (check terracotta-on-cream and every stone mid-tone in BOTH themes)" — DESIGN-RULES, Accessibility minimums.
- **Evidence**: `p4b-contrast` output (light and dark), `p4-report.json`.
- **Suspected location**: the stone ramp (translucent ink) and `cream` on `terracotta-500` in `app/globals.css`; brand.md's "cream text on it".
- **Smallest possible patch**: raise stone-400/500 opacity until 4.5:1 on ground (≈ ink at 62% / 72%), lift stone-300 where it carries words (nav inactive, future rows) to stone-400, and either darken the CTA fill toward terracotta-600 or set CTA text to ink. Each is a palette decision under #165/#203, so it is **not applied here**; logged in `docs/devibe/needs-judgment.md` with the ratios.

## Hold this in your hands
